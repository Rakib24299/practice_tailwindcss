About Social Media

Facebook

Facebook is a global social networking service founded in 2004, now owned by Meta Platforms, with over 3 billion monthly active users worldwide.

Instragram

Instagram is a global social media platform for sharing photos and short videos, owned by Meta Platforms, with over 2 billion monthly active users.

Linkedin

LinkedIn is the world’s largest professional networking platform, connecting over 1.2 billion members globally to enhance career development and business opportunities.

Whatsapp

WhatsApp is a widely used messaging and calling app that allows users to communicate via text, voice, video, and media sharing across mobile and desktop platforms.
